Please join us on Memorial day at the picturesque Battery Gardens Restaurant, to celebrate our 4th Annual Fundraising Gala. All proceeds from this Gala will support our mission to provide college education opportunities for underprivileged youth with leadership qualities living in the developing world and to support local community based organizations with programs related to poverty reduction. We are proud to have been able to offer overseas education opportunities to students across several countries but we want to take this to the next level and provide a US education to these talented young individuals but cannot do so without your support and contributions.

The 4th Annual Founder’s Gala

This year’s Gala will be held at Battery Gardens Restaurant ( http://batterygardens.com ), a Manhattan Oasis with sweeping panoramic views of glorious New York Harbor, including historic Ellis Island and The Statue of Liberty. What better way to honor Memorial day and support a worthwhile cause? We have a memorable and fun evening planned, so please join us!!
