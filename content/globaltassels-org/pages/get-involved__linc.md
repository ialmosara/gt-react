# Learning and Innovative Communities (LInC)

Source: https://globaltassels.org/get-involved/linc/
Last modified: 2024-11-28

The LInC Program is a transformative initiative designed to empower non-governmental organizations (NGOs) to become integral partners in the Global Tassels community. These partnerships are focused on missions that align with key areas such as youth development, educational access, social mobility, social awareness, and poverty reduction.

Through the LInC Program, Global Tassels collaborates with its NGO partners to provide financial assistance and resources, enabling them to scale their missions effectively within their local communities. This initiative not only supports the NGOs but also creates opportunities for Global Tassels Student Leaders to actively participate and contribute.

By engaging in these partnerships, Global Tassels addresses its mission of community engagement in multiple ways. The organization offers volunteers, provides expert advice, secures necessary resources, and grants financial assistance to strengthen the impact of its NGO partners. This holistic approach ensures that Global Tassels and its partners can drive meaningful change and promote sustainable development in communities around the world.
