# PROJECT SEE “Student Educational Endeavors”

Source: https://globaltassels.org/fundraising/project-see-student-educational-endeavors/
Last modified: 2022-04-19

Selecting worthy young leaders eager to change the world is simply not enough. Our commitment to our Student Leaders must have longevity. The organization support must be specific to their educational, developmental, and community needs. Since failure is not an option, PROJECT SEE will enable funding support day by day, month by month, and year after year. Our goal is to give donors a unique opportunity to support Global Tassels’ Student Leaders by dedicating financial and in-kind resources in their own name, the name of their loved ones or to honor any family members. It is a scholarship-naming occasion for those who we consider as Major Donors. With this, it requires us to have collective and consistent focus to permanently empower our Student Leaders. Finally, this is a visionary mean by which donors can shape the future of the world, one individual at a time, providing major financial support to complete higher educational endeavors.
