# PROJECT AIR “Academia In Reach”

Source: https://globaltassels.org/recruitment/global-community-project/
Last modified: 2022-04-19

Project AIR is Global Tassels’ visionary leadership and academic development program, open to service-minded students living in disadvantaged communities in the developing world. In close collaboration with community partners, we reach out to identify GT Student Leaders through a rigorous selection process. We provide GT Student Leaders with the infrastructure (counseling, support network, career training, language and cultural competencies) so they will succeed within our partnering academic institutions. Upon reentry back into their local communities, GT Student Leaders are required to engage in a two-year service commitment to support local initiatives to end poverty.
