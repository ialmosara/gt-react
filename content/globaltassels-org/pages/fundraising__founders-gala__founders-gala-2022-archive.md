# Founder’s Gala 2022 Archive

Source: https://globaltassels.org/fundraising/founders-gala/founders-gala-2022-archive/
Last modified: 2023-04-30

Please join us on July 1st, 2022 at 6pm EST USA online via Zoom to celebrate our 8th Annual Fundraising Gala. All proceeds from this Gala will support our mission to provide college education opportunities for underprivileged youth with leadership qualities living in the developing world and to support local community-based organizations with programs related to poverty reduction. We are proud to have been able to offer overseas education opportunities to students across several countries but we want to take this to the next level and provide a US education to these talented young individuals but cannot do so without your support and contributions.
