NOMINATE A POTENTIAL STUDENT

Potential Student Jamaica September 15 – October 20

Nominations is for a student who will receive a full college scholarship through Global Tassels — awarded to a high-achieving leader who demonstrates exceptional promise but requires financial assistance to pursue higher education. Our mission is to remove financial barriers and invest in resilient students who embody academic excellence, service, and the courage to lead change in their communities.

The International Recruitment Course of Action (TIRCA) of Global Tassels is an official document that describes our initial work to our pilot countries up to our candidate’s final selection process.

This page is committed solely for Community Leaders representing community based- organizations, government sectors, businesses, religious organizations, and NGOs to nominate potential students to Global Tassels.

Please note that nominations for each country have their own timeline. Nomination forms will only open during this time.
