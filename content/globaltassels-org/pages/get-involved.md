# Community Engagements

Source: https://globaltassels.org/get-involved/
Last modified: 2022-04-19

DEPARTMENT MISSION

To engage in practice and integrate meaningful community services with Global Tassels Community Partners.

To establish service-learning initiatives that will empower individuals with civic responsibilities.

To provide resources to GT student leaders and GT community partners.

To maintain and enhance a purpose centered GT community projects tailored to poverty reduction.

DEPARTMENT VISION

To have a global pattern of annual community services and positive human engagements by us and our community partners.

If you want to learn more about our local, national, or global community services, please contact Mr. Haronid “Yogi” Javier, Chief Operating Officer at yog@globaltassels.org
