The 11th Annual Founder’s Gala

Please join us on Saturday, August 16, 2025 in Washington, DC to celebrate our 11th Annual Founder Fundraising Gala. All proceeds from this Gala will support our mission to provide college education opportunities for underprivileged youth with leadership qualities living in the developing world and to support local community-based organizations with programs related to poverty reduction. We are proud to have been able to offer overseas education opportunities to students across several countries but we want to take this to the next level and provide a US education to these talented young individuals but cannot do so without your support and contributions.

This year’s Gala will be celebrated in Washington, DC in person.

11th Annual Founder Fundraising Gala

Be a changemaker and support our vision to build a network of change agents working to eradicate poverty around the world.
