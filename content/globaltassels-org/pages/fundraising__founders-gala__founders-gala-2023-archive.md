# Founder’s Gala 2023 Archive

Source: https://globaltassels.org/fundraising/founders-gala/founders-gala-2023-archive/
Last modified: 2024-02-05

Please join us on August 6th, 2023 at 12pm EST USA online via Zoom to celebrate our 9th Annual Fundraising Gala. All proceeds from this Gala will support our mission to provide college education opportunities for underprivileged youth with leadership qualities living in the developing world and to support local community-based organizations with programs related to poverty reduction. We are proud to have been able to offer overseas education opportunities to students across several countries but we want to take this to the next level and provide a US education to these talented young individuals but cannot do so without your support and contributions.

Please join us on August 6th, 2023 at 12pm EST New York online on Zoom to celebrate our 9th Annual Fundraising Gala. All proceeds from this Gala will support our mission to provide college education opportunities for underprivileged youth with leadership qualities living in the developing world and to support local community-based organizations with programs related to poverty reduction. We are proud to have been able to offer overseas education opportunities to students across several countries but we want to take this to the next level and provide a US education to these talented young individuals but cannot do so without your support and contributions.

An Annual Benefit event for our Student-Leaders College Education and our Global Community Projects

Be a changemaker and support our vision to build a network of change agents working to eradicate poverty around the world.
