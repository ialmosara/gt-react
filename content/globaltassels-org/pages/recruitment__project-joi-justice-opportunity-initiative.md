# PROJECT JOI “Justice & Opportunity Initiative”

Source: https://globaltassels.org/recruitment/project-joi-justice-opportunity-initiative/
Last modified: 2022-04-19

As an organization, Global Tassels’ commitment towards commitment towards community development is central to the United Nation’s Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). In GT, the Sustainable Development Goals are personified, in turn; by GT Student Leaders’ own commitments to lead the fight against extreme poverty in their home countries. During their studies, GT Student Leaders are given the foundation and the framework to start a hub for innovation to stimulate world of ideas. This center will be the mantle for equal justice and opportunity against any forms of poverty. It will be open for everyone in their local communities. The idea is for GT Student Leaders to lead Project JOI with their own vision and creative endeavors. GT supports their community development, from inception to completion. GT’s model for community development embraces innovation ideas for change aligned by the SDGs and targeting specific constituents where GT Student Leaders believe its most needed.
