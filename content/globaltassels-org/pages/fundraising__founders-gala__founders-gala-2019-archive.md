Source: https://globaltassels.org/fundraising/founders-gala/founders-gala-2019-archive/
Last modified: 2022-04-19

Please join us on June 1st 2019 at The Prince George Ballroom in heart of New York City’s Flatiron District, to celebrate our 5th Annual Fundraising Gala. All proceeds from this Gala will support our mission to provide college education opportunities for underprivileged youth with leadership qualities living in the developing world and to support local community-based organizations with programs related to poverty reduction. We are proud to have been able to offer overseas education opportunities to students across several countries but we want to take this to the next level and provide a US education to these talented young individuals but cannot do so without your support and contributions.

We have limited attendance available so please book your tickets today!

This year’s Gala will be held at The Prince George Ballroom (www.princegeorgeballroom.org), an elegant Victorian Rennasiance venue. The Gala will have a platform of honorees known for their hard work and commitment to change lives, whether from their holistic creative contribution or their personal values to make our world a better place to live in. GT will honor them because they are not only champions of courage but also champions of change. Merit alone did not get them to where they are as – they are our heroes for being fearless enough to start something and make a difference -no matter what, where, and how that may look like. The 2019 Founder’s Gala in New York City will be a celebration to remember.
