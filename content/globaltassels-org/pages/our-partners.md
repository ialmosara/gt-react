# Our Partners

Source: https://globaltassels.org/our-partners/
Last modified: 2025-10-22

Global Tassels, Inc. partners with corporations and organizations dedicated to helping end global poverty through education and other programs. Our organization enables leading companies to have a lasting impact on global communities and youth seeking to end the poverty gap through community service and scholarship.

Community partners help build stronger communities in our Student Leaders’ home countries and support the Global Tassels mission in providing quality education effective poverty reduction programs.

Please note that while we support and team up with organizations in the US or abroad, Global Tassels do not endorse the views they express or recommend any product or services they offer.

WHY PARTNER WITH GLOBAL TASSELS

Our experienced team will work with you to create a targeted, strategic partnership that meets your community and business objectives. We will be your best-in-class partner, ensuring that you accomplish your goals, leverage your company’s mission and make a difference in the Global Community. Partnership benefits include:

Impact in the Global Community: Your partnership and contribution will go towards the Global Tassels’ mission of ending global poverty through education and our commitment to service/community engagement.

Community Leadership and Service: We will help provide appropriate GT Leaders to act as volunteers for your organization.

Recognition: You will receive public recognition for your support here on our website and during our special events.

Community Partners are often invited to join GT Delegations on annual Mission Trips.

and most importantly, you become part of the Global Tassels Family.
